Mission rules & progression
  • Mixed: 12 balanced practice decisions plus 4 boss decisions. A domain route covers its 15 practice decisions plus 1 boss decision. New mixed runs vary the selection.
  • Choose an answer, rate confidence 1–3, then lock it. Read why each option wins or loses before advancing. No timer, lives, or penalties for taking time.
  • A miss can return once, after at least two other decisions. Late and boss misses wait for a new Replay missed session. A correct retry clears that unresolved miss, not its history.
  • Correct decisions earn 100 XP plus a streak bonus of 20–100 XP. Cadet → Analyst at 1,000 → Defender at 3,000 → Sentinel at 6,000 XP. Ranks reward practice, not exam readiness.
  • BLACKOUT: earn one shield per correct boss decision. Secure every available shield to earn a deterministic 200 XP finale bonus. No random damage or hidden pass threshold.

Choose a route and launch when ready. Your last mission resumes automatically when you return.

About the learning design

A formative study aid, not graded coursework, official exam questions, or a complete CISSP preparation course. These original prompts sample Domains 1–4, not Domains 5–8.

  • Retrieval before feedback: commit a decision before seeing the explanation. Roediger & Karpicke (2006).
  • Practice testing and distributed practice: Dunlosky et al. (2013). Two intervening questions create a small within-session lag, not a validated long-term spacing schedule. Return on later days too.
  • Confidence and corrective feedback: review confident errors and low-confidence correct answers; a correct guess is not secure knowledge. Butler, Karpicke & Roediger (2008). The 1–3 scale and labels here are practical reflection aids, not a validated diagnostic.
  • Scope: Current official ISC2 CISSP exam outline (linked page lists the April 15, 2024 effective outline). Verify the official outline for updates; this game is not affiliated with ISC2.
  • Network foundations: Computer Networks: A Systems Approach — Reliable Transmission.
